TRAVEL

Afternoon Light
in Positano

MAY 18, 2024 • ITALY • 4 MIN READ

Positano doesn’t reveal itself immediately. It asks you to climb, to wander, and to stop for coffee far more often than you had planned. Arriving in the late afternoon, the cliffside village turns golden, casting long lavender shadows across the sparkling sea.

The streets are steep and winding, the houses stacked like waves. Sit in the afternoon light; everything slows down. Linen dries on balconies, the sea sparkles, and life feels beautifully simple.
